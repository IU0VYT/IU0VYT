4.11.0 (08/11/2026)
-------------------
- Zone names (zonenames.go): instead of «Zone 1», «Zone 2»… every zone has an editable
  name («✎ Zone names…» or double click on the zone of the bar). Formats derived from
  the code of the manufacturer's programs and from CHIRP's driver: Radtel RT-950 Pro /
  Bajeton BJ-9000 / Hiroyasu HI-UV850 Pro (RT-950PRO_CPS v1.2.0-v1.3.3, BJ-9000_CPS
  v1.3.3) and Radtel RT-950 (BT-9000_CPS v1.3.0): area 0xC000, 16 bytes per zone, 12
  GB2312 bytes padded with 0xFF; Radtel RT-920 (RT-920_EnCPS v1.2.4 and CHIRP's
  radtel_rt900.py): area 0xC800, 10 zones, 10 characters as in CHIRP; Radtel RT-910 /
  Bajeton BJ-7700 without Bluetooth (RT910_EnCPS v1.0.3): area 0xA200, 12 ASCII
  characters. DMR radios RD-5R, DM-1801 and DM-32UV: names of the existing zones, 16
  characters. The area 0xC000-0xC0FF is now read on the BJ-9000 / RT-950 Pro and, last
  and optional, on the RT-950; it is written only if the names change, after reading
  it back from the radio, and only if it contains names or is empty (otherwise nothing
  is written); RT-950 files saved before 4.11 do not touch the area. Tests with the
  simulated radios (zonenames_test.go).
- Bulk edit of the ticked or selected channels (radio.js): power, bandwidth, scan, TX
  tone, RX tone, shift and transmission (receive only), with tones and shift checked
  before changing the channels and a single undo step.
- Languages: Korean (flag of South Korea) and Arabic (flag of the United Arab
  Emirates; page right to left, numbers and frequencies left to right; Windows message
  boxes right to left), 4902 texts per language; information files translated in
  lingue/ko and lingue/ar.
- Site: the available languages on the page; the sentence listing the firmware files
  has been removed.
- Tests: i18n_test.go compares the path with filepath.ToSlash (go test passes on
  Windows); new documenti_test.go: the copies of LEGGIMI.txt, AUTORI_E_LICENZE.txt and
  the license in «sorgenti» must be identical to those of the main folder (the
  «sorgenti» copy of LEGGIMI.txt had fallen behind in 4.10).
