4.20.0 (11/11/2026)
-------------------
- Radtel RT-4D and RT-6D (DMR) and RT-1000 Pro (drv_rt4d.go, new), from the
  manufacturer's programs («RT-4D CPS» 2.07, «DMR RT-6D» 2.06, «Radtel RT-1000 Pro CPS»
  of 6 August 2026), one generation after the Bajeton DM-5000 one: same link (115200
  baud, 1 KiB blocks), different channel and zone. 48-byte channel with bit fields in
  the first five bytes, frequencies in tens of Hz at bytes 5-12, tones at bytes 13-16,
  name at bytes 32-47; 250 zones of 520 bytes with up to 250 channels. Block 8
  (password), blocks 16-63 (channels) and 0x78-0xF7 (zones) are read; writing follows
  the order of the program and stops after the zones: 0x90 (settings) and 0x92 (VFO)
  with the data just read from the radio, 0x91 (48 channel blocks), 0x93 (128 zone
  blocks). The items of an existing channel that are not handled are preserved; between
  108 and 136 MHz the channel is written in AM.
- Not done because not certain: the channels selected for A/B/C in the zone (the program
  saves them one way and reads them back another: those bytes are preserved); on the
  RT-1000 Pro the scan exclusion (the program writes it in one bit and reads it from
  another: it is not handled, the byte stays as it is).
- Catalogue (catalog_420.go, new): 3 entries. Counts: 502 radios, 427 with settings, 556
  in the gallery. Translations: 13 new texts.

4.19.0 (11/11/2026)
-------------------
- Radtel RT-817A / RT-871A (drv_iradio.go, kind irRT871A), from the manufacturer's
  program («Radtel RT-871A CPS», same code base as the RT-860 one, compared method by
  method): same programming entry, same 1 KiB blocks and same 48-byte channel as the
  RT-880G, without the zones. Blocks 8-11 (settings), 0x10-0x3F (1024 channels) and
  0x358 (FM radio) are read; areas 0x90 (4 blocks), 0x91 (48) and 0x99 (1) are written;
  the logo is not handled. Power 0 low, 1 medium, 2 high; the 12-byte channel code is
  preserved; limits 18-660 and 840-1000 MHz.
- Catalogue (catalog_419.go, new): 1 entry. Counts: 499 radios, 427 with settings, 553
  in the gallery. Translations: 3 new texts.
- Examined and not done: Radtel RT-4D, RT-6D and RT-1000 Pro (programs of a newer
  generation with a different area layout: a new driver is needed); Kenwood TK-2180 /
  TK-3180 (tk8180.py builds the memory format from the zones and uses a file with its
  own header: it cannot be reduced to a simple edit of the existing channels).

4.18.0 (11/11/2026)
-------------------
- Kenwood TK-2140 and TK-3140 (drv_kwtk140.go, new; CHIRP's tk3140.py: TK-2140K,
  TK-3140K, TK-3140K2, TK-3140K3) and Kenwood TK-280 and TK-380 (drv_kwtk280.go, new;
  CHIRP's tk280.py): 250 positions of 48 bytes divided into zones or groups. The
  program's list is the list of physical positions: an edited channel stays in its zone
  with its number, a new channel goes into the zone of the channel before it with the
  first free number; the position table is rebuilt as in CHIRP.
- Comparison tool with CHIRP (verifica/chirp/chirpref.py): it now handles the radios
  that have one «sub-radio» per zone in CHIRP. Reading, 15 edits, memory layout, bytes
  exchanged and final memory are identical to CHIRP for the six models. Settings: 6 new
  classes (314 in all); the 750 per-group entries of the TK-280/380 are not included.
- Quansheng UV-K5 / UV-K6 with fagci's R3b0rn firmware (drv_fagci.go, new), from the
  firmware sources: settings, VFOs and 40 presets at 0x0000-0x052E; 23-byte channels at
  the end of the EEPROM going backwards; EEPROM type in the low three bits of the first
  byte (8 to 256 KiB: 298, 654, 678 or 1024 channels); 32-bit addresses, writes in
  groups of 8 bytes, 38400 baud. Only the channel area is written. Refused: firmware
  that does not answer the 32-bit read, a number of presets other than 40, memory not
  prepared, memory of a different size. The record was also checked by compiling the
  firmware's structures. Intended differences from the CHIRP driver published with the
  firmware: the transmit frequency is always written in full, names of 9 characters.
- Radtel RT-330 and RT-400 (drv_jc8810.go), from the manufacturer's programs («Radio
  CPS» and «JJCC-7700 CPS»), which use the protocol and channel record of the MML
  JC-8810 family. RT-330: handshake «PROGRAMYF68U», 115200 baud, 512 channels, 100-520
  MHz. RT-400: handshake «PROGRAMJC81U» (that of the RT-470), 57600 baud, 256 channels,
  18-1000 MHz; the manufacturer's program does not check the identifier, so the tab
  refuses the identifiers of the other models with the same handshake. Tested only with
  a simulated radio.
- Catalogue (catalog_418.go, new): 4 entries, and the R3b0rn tab on the UV-K5. Counts:
  498 radios, 427 with settings, 552 in the gallery. Translations: 29 new texts.
- Not done in this version: Kenwood TK-2180 / TK-3180 (in CHIRP every addition or
  deletion moves the whole memory); Radtel RT-871A, RT-1000 Pro, RT-4D, RT-6D, RT-5D
  (areas or records different from those already handled).

4.17.0 (11/11/2026)
-------------------
- Kenwood TH-F6A, TH-F7E, TH-G71A/E, TH-D7A/E, TH-D7A(G)/E(G), TH-K2 (drv_kwlive.go,
  new; commands of CHIRP's kenwood_live.py and kenwood_d7.py): radio switched on
  normally, 9600 baud, one channel at a time; own image format; the fields the program
  does not change stay as the radio gave them; step and mode stay until the frequency
  changes; when writing, the radio is read again and only the changed channels are sent.
  Tested only with a simulated radio.
- Kenwood professional handhelds of the 60 series (drv_kwtk60.go, new; CHIRP's tk270.py:
  TK-260, TK-270, TK-272, TK-278, TK-360, TK-370, TK-372, TK-378) and of the 60G series
  (CHIRP's tk760g.py: TK-260G, TK-270G, TK-272G, TK-278G, TK-360G, TK-370G, TK-372G,
  TK-378G, TK-388G). Intended differences from CHIRP: busy channel lockout, beat shift,
  group and position of an edited channel stay as they were.
- Radtel RT-860 (catalogue): Radtel's program uses the same commands, the same blocks
  and the same 48-byte channel as the CHIRP driver of the RT-880G, so the RT-860 uses
  that tab (comparison with the manufacturer's program, not tested on a real radio).
- Catalogue: 494 radios, 425 with settings, 548 in the gallery, 47 brands. Settings: 17
  new classes. Translations: 13 new texts. Tests: simulated radios for the three new
  Kenwood families; comparison with CHIRP for the 60 and 60G series.

4.13.1 (09/11/2026)
-------------------
- IJV V6 firmware (drv_ijv6.go, new): tab «Quansheng UV-K5 V3 / UV-K1 (IJV V6
  firmware)». V6 data link (DTR and RTS low; «A5 5A» request with CRC32, 12-byte
  «5A A5» reply): info and version, opening with the announced number of requests,
  256-byte reads, 256-byte pages committed in 4 KiB sectors, end and reset. Same
  memory as V5 (bank n at 0x020000 + n·0x4000, active bank from «CFG5», read again
  before writing). Only the changed sectors are written, each read back and compared;
  calibration, licence and the areas the radio manages itself stay as on the radio.
- The other IJV tabs point to the V6 tab when the radio answers only the V6 data link.
- Settings: V6 uses the V5 table. Catalogue and UV-K5 firmware: V6 tab added.
- Translations: 23 new or changed texts. Tests: simulated V6 radio (read, write of the
  changed sectors only, changed bank, refused settings, wrong tab).

4.13.0 (09/11/2026)
-------------------
- IJV firmware for Quansheng UV-K5 / UV-K5 V3 / UV-K1 (drv_ijv.go, new): four tabs,
  «IJV V3 firmware, 200 channels» (channels at 0x0500, 8 KiB image), «V3 X, 999
  channels» (0x2000-0x9FFF), «IJV V4 firmware» and «IJV V5 firmware» (500 channels of
  the bank selected on the radio, 24 KiB image: fixed area 0x0000-0x1FFF and bank at
  0x2000-0x5FFF). 32-byte channel record; group, AGC, compander, scrambler, selective
  code, TX lock and busy lock are kept. Link: V3 with the UV-K5 factory protocol; V4
  and FW5 with the plain IJV protocol and, if the radio does not answer, the factory
  one; FW5 with physical read/write (0x0564/0x0566) and the final save (0x0576).
  Active bank from byte 0x000B (V4) or from the «CFG5» configuration (FW5): it is read
  again before writing and, if another bank is selected on the radio, nothing is
  written. V4 and FW5 write only the changed blocks; calibration (from 0x1E00), FW5
  configuration and licence never. V6 is recognised by its data link and the program
  explains that it is managed with IJV's RadioManager. Formats derived from the CHIRP
  modules published by IJV (3.60, V4.0, V5.0) and from the analysis of RadioManager
  4.3.2; code written from scratch.
- IJV settings (settings_ijv.go, new): main menus, display, VOX, side keys (V3 and
  V4), names of the 16 groups and (V4, FW5) of the 16 banks.
- UV-K5 firmware (fwk5.go): a .bin file with an IJV version points to the IJV tab for
  the channels, with a warning (V5 and V6 also need SYSTEM and modules, which only
  RadioManager loads).
- Windows serial port: DTR can be switched off (SetDTR), for the V6 data link.
- Catalogue: the IJV tabs in the Quansheng UV-K5 and UV-K1, with the IJV website.
- Translations: 143 new texts in all languages.
- Tests: simulated IJV radios (V3, V4, FW5, V6), plain and factory protocol, FW5
  announcements, changed bank, refused write, calibration never written.
